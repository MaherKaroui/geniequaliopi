# Architecture rules

- VTest and Product Builder needs-assessment drafts use a shared pathway-aware answer helper before generic branches; exact pathway titles select level/block content, certification-only inputs never infer a level, missing Product Builder qualifications use explicitly unverified profile proposals, and declared qualifications and recorded LMS answers still override each drafted answer.

- IZILearn preserves exact AcroForm contract values through its learner adapter and applies them last in the shared CERFA generator, excluding OPCO-reserved fields; this keeps contract data authoritative without changing other documents.
- CFA mission QCM and J-1 announcement mails expose structured MailAuto builders and share the catalog mail resolver with standard mails for every capture/export path; this prevents separate Gmail shells and preserves mission scheduling.
- The shared captureMail boundary applies Gmail's Ctrl+E state idempotently and removes the editor toolbar from the disposable iframe before measuring/rendering; Gmail hidden/export CSS also excludes it from layout, preventing full-height captures from rendering a translated black bar across Qualiopi and IZILearn.

- Database-backed formation presets use exact title/code identity, a shared in-memory registry and server-owned checkpoint/lease operations; pedagogical corrections never populate learner evidence, and explicit IZILearn lessons retain priority.
- IZILearn owns a presentation boundary for booklet supplements and document revision footers, excluding email captures, reuses the shared dossier design controls, and passes a local MAJ override into its context; session/evidence dates remain unchanged and all preview/export paths use the same resolved context.
- Qualiopi and IZILearn reuse attendancePresentation for IND 11 tables; IZILearn prefills planned schedules (explicit slots first, Qualiopi blocks as fallback). Past days without recorded evidence auto-fill like Qualiopi ("Signé X.Nom" or reusable signature, buildRealisticConnectionDay times, "Présent(e)"); future days stay blank; recorded slot signatures and connection logs always win; connection signatures stay below tables.

- IZILearn's shared piece catalog uses Qualiopi generators and final exam questions; explicit LMS exam content/results take priority and missing exam results stay blank. Only substantive responses override autofill; future evaluations stay blank and planned schedules never become attendance or connection evidence. Previews resolve pieces by ID to avoid stale content.
- IZILearn initial placement renders proposed copies through one preview/export boundary, with ranked QCM combinations and shared corrected open-answer personalization; recorded quiz results bypass proposals and empty preset open-question arrays fall back to formation-specific questions.
- IZILearn email drafts reuse the shared Qualiopi scenarios and Gmail/Outlook capture engine for previews and PNG exports; the capture adapter includes only organizer messages without injecting status text into their body, missing addresses never suppress drafts, and synthetic meeting links are replaced with session-access instructions to avoid fabricated evidence. Generated captures do not record or confirm actual delivery.
- IZILearn signatures are captured beside each learner (and their company representative when present), saved in browser localStorage (izilearn-signatures) and keyed by organization/session/enrollment/contact/identity/role; explicit reusable learner images may also arrive in the payload. They fill shared Qualiopi fields, unsigned form boxes and recorded-connection cells marked as copied signatures, never replacing existing signatures, signing missing attendance evidence or inventing electronic-signing timestamps. Slot SVGs remain scoped to their recorded attendance slot.

- IZIBUSINESS completed training records and dates are resolved by one shared helper for the combined attestation and every IND 22 plan, preventing planned dates from overwriting completed actions.

- Rapid document edits are sanitized HTML overrides stored inside the rapid formation JSON and resolved at the shared preview/export boundary; identity-based recipient/action keys prevent corrections leaking to another person or action, and the account-scoped local draft survives browser restarts without changing database schema.

- Rapid generation enables `FormationInfo.dureeSaisiePrioritaire` at its shared preparation boundary; duration helpers honor it to preserve entered duration without changing other generators.
- VAE contractual articles are generated through the shared `genVAEContrat` for indicators 4 and 9; company presence selects the parties while the declared funding selects payment terms, avoiding divergent documents.
- External quality-services contracts use the shared `genContratPrestationQualiopi` across action types, distinct from trainer and beneficiary contracts, so mandate clauses and provider identity cannot diverge.
- Only `/vae?embed=1` and `/bilan-competences?embed=1` mount their standalone presentations outside AccessGate, without global navigation or chat; their dossier histories live only in browser localStorage (no account, no backend write); they never grant persistent access or change authentication/RLS, so embedding does not unlock other tools or expose saved records. AI generation retains its existing authenticated server access.
- Embedded VAE presentation overrides semantic tokens only inside its page wrapper; normal tools and generated document styles remain independent of the host-app appearance.
- Standalone VAE exports use template-local PDF margins and opt into row-by-row table pagination; the shared export default remains unchanged to isolate layout corrections from other generators.
- The standalone BDC generator uses a dedicated typed dossier and export catalog around the existing BDC legal/financial model; explicit declarations and confirmed sessions replace inferred profiles to avoid fabricated evidence, and its separate browser history never changes access rights.
- Standalone BDC AI drafting runs in an authenticated server function and fills narrative fields, questionnaire answers and tables directly into documents (no AI disclaimer, like VAE); financial, identity, scheduling, presence and signature data remain unchanged, and session reports only target held, presence-confirmed sessions.
- BDC final-result-only AI output retains SDK strict schema and final validation but disables unused partial-object parsing to avoid quadratic CPU work on long streamed drafts.- The four built-in TP transport/accounting/payroll presets live in formationPresets/builtinTP (static JSON) and join the shared preset registry, so every generator reuses them; database presets still take priority by rank.
- The two LILATE presets (English RS7314, FLE RS6916) are built from CRM LMS lessons by scripts/build-lilate-presets.mjs into static JSON, live in formationPresets/builtinLilate and join the same registry (exact titles/aliases or RS code); their course chapters feed the shared support generator, and corrigés never become learner evidence.
- LILATE learner copies (EN RS7314/RS6118, FLE RS6916) come from lilateHumanized: one CECRL level per learner rank drives QCM scores, item-by-item open answers derived from each real question's corrigé (explanations stripped, typical errors), oral/written exercise productions and needs answers; a per-rank comment bank keeps copies distinct. RS6118 (inactive since 24/10/2025) reuses the RS7314 content for earlier sessions only.
